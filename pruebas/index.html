Executive Summary

We are not proposing more events. We are proposing an adoption path.

Most growth initiatives stop at awareness or registration. This proposal is designed to move people from first contact with Digital Finance to real, responsible use of the Bybit ecosystem.

Bybit can differentiate in Peru not only through marketing, but through applied financial education, meaningful product use and a sustainable local community.
