Executive Summary

Not another one-off event. A measurable path to adoption.

Most crypto activations stop at awareness or account registration. This proposal is designed to guide participants from first contact with Digital Finance to meaningful use of the Bybit ecosystem.

The pilot combines university initiatives, coworking activations, educational workshops, practical product demonstrations, recurring touchpoints, and a final community event. The objective is to create a repeatable community growth model that can later expand beyond Lima.

Bybit can differentiate itself in Peru not only through marketing, but through applied financial education, real product usage, and a sustainable local community.
Strategic Opportunity

Peru offers room for both community growth and category expansion.

University MarketStudents and young professionals already recognize leading exchanges. The opportunity is to convert visibility into continuity, financial education, community leadership, and real product adoption.
Professional & Entrepreneur MarketCoworking spaces concentrate founders, freelancers, executives, and globally minded professionals who need access to digital payments, stablecoins, global liquidity, and investment tools.
Business Use CasesThere is an emerging opportunity around treasury management, stablecoins, international transfers, liquidity, Earn products, and collateralized financing.
Lifestyle CommunitiesHigh-value interest groups such as motorsports, motorcycle clubs, premium sports, and entrepreneur communities offer access to audiences with purchasing power, performance awareness, and investment capacity.

90-Day Pilot

A structured journey from discovery to active usage

Month 1Discovery & Trust

Digital Finance fundamentals, security, stablecoins, P2P, Bybit ecosystem, registration, and KYC.

Month 2Activation

Funding, Earn, payments, liquidity, Bybit Card, Spot trading, and practical use cases.

Month 3Adoption & Retention

Portfolio construction, diversification, responsible trading, risk management, and continued product usage.

Closing WeekBybit Community Night

Recognition, certification, networking, leaders, ambassadors, referrals, and next-stage community development.

21 adoption touchpoints across 3 universities, 3 coworking spaces, 1 business pilot, and 1 final community event.
